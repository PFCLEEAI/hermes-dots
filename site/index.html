What it does / v1

Context that carries forward.

01

Remembers with sources

Markdown notes, searchable memory, and redacted logs. Orca searches its brain before each task and cites the notes it uses. Your edits win.

02

Follows up thoughtfully

Check-backs come with a time and a reason. Questions come with a recommendation. Other allowed work continues, and nudges respect quiet hours.

03

Watches the inbox

Urgent pings, regular-mail digests, and reply drafts for you to copy. v1 uses practice mail and a mock-tested Gmail read-only adapter. No email sending.

04

Changes settings by chat

“Make quiet hours 22 to 7.” “Digest only at 9.” Orca confirms the new value, then saves it and shows what was stored.

05

Stays within your rules

One owner, an isolated home, Docker for code, and approvals for specific outside actions. Pause or stop from Slack whenever you need to.

How it works

From a message to a next step.

  1. Give it a responsibility

    Tell Orca what you need in Slack. Review a card with the goal, allowed sources, actions, and evidence needed to finish.

  2. Confirm the plan

    Orca works within that card, searches memory, and schedules check-backs. Anything needing your approval waits for your yes.

  3. Keep the thread moving

    Get updates and digests with sources, open questions, and the next check time. Completed work comes with evidence.

Quickstart

A home of its own.

macOS first. Python 3.11+, Git, Docker Desktop, your Slack workspace, and your chosen Hermes model provider.

Read the installation guide ↗
git clone https://github.com/PFCLEEAI/hermes-dots.git
cd hermes-dots
python3 scripts/install.py --home ~/.hermes-dots
  1. Install pinned Hermes in the separate dots home.
  2. Connect one Slack app and configure yourself as the sole owner.
  3. Finish onboarding in chat; confirm “Watch the practice inbox.”

v1 setup contract; implementation is in progress. Gmail connection comes later, with gmail.readonly only. The login item is generated for you to enable.

Safety

You keep the say.

Boundaries before actions

The stock ~/.hermes home stays denied. Code runs in non-persistent Docker containers without credential mounts. Sending, deletion outside scratch, spending, and account changes ask first; approvals are specific and single-use.

Memory you can inspect

Notes are Markdown. Logs are redacted and append-only. Forget removes notes and search entries and suppresses re-learning, while retained logs remain. Skill and prompt changes stay proposals until you approve.

Only needed excerpts go to your configured model provider. Slack receives cards and digests. Local and mock tests do not establish live account operation. Read the safety model.
